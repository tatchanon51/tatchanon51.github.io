8. Closing Note

💡 Key Takeaway

August 2026 showed us that volume is recovering, but conversion is the real battle. The September plan prioritizes fixing KKT ขอนแก่น's pipeline and scaling สยามนิสสัน อุบล's NMT-activity model network-wide. We don't need to invent new strategies — we need to execute the proven ones more consistently across the dealer network.

Three Things We Must Do Differently in September

  1. Stop collecting cold leads. KKT HO's 80% Cold rate is a red flag. Every prospect must get a follow-up call within 48 hours, or we lose them.
  2. Invest in NMT activities, not just dealer activities. Ubon's data shows 67% of RS comes from NMT events. This is the highest-ROI channel — double down on it.
  3. Track weekly, not monthly. With RS at 6/month for KKT HO, one bad week can wreck the month. Weekly check-ins on PP→TD conversion rate are critical.

Target for end of September: 400 retail sales network-wide, with KKT HO contributing at least 10 units. This is ambitious but achievable if we execute on the priorities above.
